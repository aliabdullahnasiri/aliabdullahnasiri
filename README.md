## 👋 About Me

Hi, I’m **Ali Abdullah** — a passionate **Cybersecurity learner and developer** with a strong focus on offensive security, networking, and automation.

I enjoy breaking down complex systems to understand how they work, how they fail, and how they can be secured. Most of my work revolves around **ethical hacking, Linux, networking, and Python-based automation**. I’m constantly learning and building hands-on projects to sharpen my real-world skills.

### 💡 What I focus on:
- Ethical Hacking & Penetration Testing  
- Networking & Wireless Security  
- Linux & System Internals  
- Python Automation & Scripting  
- Security Research & Tool Development  

### 🚀 My goal:
To become a highly skilled cybersecurity professional by mastering practical, real-world attack and defense techniques — not just theory.

📌 I believe in **learning by doing**, clean documentation, and writing tools that actually solve problems.
